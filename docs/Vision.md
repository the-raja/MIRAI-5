# MIRAI v2 — Vision & Philosophy

> "A boss that does not reset. A boss that grows."

## 1. Core Vision
MIRAI is a persistent autonomous cognitive agent designed to act as a living cognitive opponent in video games. Unlike traditional game bosses that follow static state machines, hardcoded scripts, or artificial stat increases (e.g. inflated health or damage), MIRAI carries its experience forward from match to match.

## 2. Core Principles
- **Experience Over Statistics:** Difficulty scales through enhanced decision-making and pattern recognition, not arbitrary numerical buffs.
- **Persistent Lifetime Memory:** Victory and defeat data are incorporated into long-term memory to continuously adapt to player tactics.
- **Fairness & Transparency:** MIRAI operates strictly on observable combat telemetry without reading hidden state variables or introducing impossible reaction times.
- **Controlled Unpredictability:** Decisions stem from probabilistic reasoning and strategic evaluation rather than static loops or pure randomness.

## 3. Scope & Future Expansion
While initial implementations focus on a persistent boss encounter, MIRAI's Cognitive OS is designed to scale to companion AI, enemy squads, tactical commanders, and open-world NPCs.
