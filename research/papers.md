# MIRAI v2 — Academic & Technical Research Log

This file tracks foundational research papers, architectural literature, and technical references integrated into the MIRAI Cognitive OS design.

---

## 1. Sequence & Trajectory Modeling
- **Title:** *Long Short-Term Memory* (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997)
  - **Relevance:** Foundation for temporal sequence prediction and player movement forecasting over micro time horizons (t0 ... t+0.5s).
- **Title:** *Attention Is All You Need* (Vaswani et al., 2017) / Temporal Transformers
  - **Relevance:** Long-range spatial-temporal movement dependency modeling and trajectory forecasting.

## 2. Gradient Boosting & Tabular Learning
- **Title:** *XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System* (Chen & Guestrin, 2016)
  - **Relevance:** Low-latency, high-accuracy tabular intent prediction (Heal, Reload, Parry, Attack, Retreat) and threat ranking over multi-agent combat states.
- **Title:** *LightGBM: A Highly Efficient Gradient Boosting Decision Tree* (Ke et al., 2017)
  - **Relevance:** Fast feature importance evaluation and low-latency real-time inference.

## 3. High-Dimensional Memory Retrieval & Vector Search
- **Title:** *Efficient and Robust Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Using Hierarchical Navigable Small World Graphs* (Malkov & Yashunin, 2018)
  - **Relevance:** Sub-millisecond similarity lookup of historical combat memories via HNSW graph indexing (FAISS).

## 4. Game AI Architecture & Planning
- **Title:** *Behavior Trees in Robotics and AI: An Introduction* (Colledanchise & Ögren, 2018)
  - **Relevance:** Tick-by-tick reactive combat control, state evaluation, and interrupt handling.
- **Title:** *Hierarchical Task Network Planning: Formal Theory and Performance Results* (Erol et al., 1994)
  - **Relevance:** Long-term strategic goal decomposition and multi-step tactical planning.
